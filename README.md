## What I care about

I believe technology should expand human agency, not reduce people to data points or replace their right to decide.

For me, **digital humanism** means building systems where:

- people can understand, own, adapt, and leave the tools they use;
- important actions remain observable, reversible, and subject to human approval;
- provenance and evidence matter as much as a successful output;
- local-first and self-hosted options remain viable where they meaningfully protect autonomy;
- maintenance, documentation, and careful review are treated as core product work.

Open source is how many of these values become practical. It is not only a licensing model to me—it is shared stewardship, public reasoning, reproducible bugs, respectful review, and responsibility to downstream users.
